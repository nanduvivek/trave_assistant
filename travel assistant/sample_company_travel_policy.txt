================================================================================
ACME GLOBAL CORP — CORPORATE TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY (2026 REVISION)
================================================================================

1. GENERAL TRAVEL POLICY OVERVIEW
This policy outlines mandatory guidelines for all employee business travel. All business travel must be booked with cost efficiency, traveler safety, and policy compliance in mind. Non-compliant expenses require written manager sign-off prior to reimbursement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. LODGING & HOTEL ACCOMMODATIONS
• Preferred Hotel Chains:
  - Primary Preferred: Marriott International (including Westin, Sheraton, Courtyard, Residence Inn, JW Marriott, Renaissance, Fairfield, SpringHill Suites, AC Hotels).
  - Secondary Preferred: Hilton Hotels (Conrad, DoubleTree, Hampton Inn, Embassy Suites).
• Non-Preferred Hotels:
  - Allowed ONLY when preferred hotels are sold out, unavailable within a 10-mile radius, or over 30% higher than local market rate.
• Nightly Rate Caps (Standard Season):
  - Tier 1 Metro Areas (New York, SF, London, Tokyo): Up to $350/night (pre-tax).
  - Tier 2 Metro Areas (Chicago, Boston, Seattle, Austin, DC): Up to $275/night (pre-tax).
  - Standard / All Other Cities: Up to $200/night (pre-tax).
• Exception Approval:
  - Any booking exceeding the nightly cap by >15% requires VP / Director sign-off (UC1 exception).
• Mobile Check-In:
  - Travelers are encouraged to utilize Marriott Bonvoy / Hilton Honors mobile key check-in to bypass front-desk delays.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. AIR TRAVEL & AIRLINE TIERS
• Airline Hierarchy:
  - Tier 1 (Most Preferred): Southwest Airlines, Delta Air Lines. (Auto-approved)
  - Tier 2 (Preferred): United Airlines, American Airlines. (Auto-approved)
  - Tier 3 (Permitted with Justification): JetBlue, Alaska Airlines, Spirit, Frontier, and regional carriers.
• Cabin Class:
  - Domestic Flights under 5 hours: Economy / Main Cabin only.
  - International Flights over 8 hours: Premium Economy or Business Class with VP approval.
• Price Matching & Total Trip Cost Rule:
  - If an employee proposes a lower-cost Tier 3 flight or alternate airport, the calculation must include Total Trip Cost:
    Base Fare + Baggage Fees ($35-$70) + Ground Transportation/Uber differences + any extra hotel night incurred.
  - If net savings is under $50, book the Tier 1/2 preferred carrier for travel protection.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. GROUND TRANSPORTATION & CAR RENTALS
• Preferred Rental Partner: Enterprise Rent-A-Car.
• Authorized Partner Network (No prior approval needed): National Car Rental, Alamo Rent A Car.
• Non-Preferred Rental Agencies: Hertz, Avis, Budget, Sixt (Requires Manager approval).
• Vehicle Class: Compact or Midsize / Intermediate only. SUVs/Luxury require business justification (e.g., transporting 3+ colleagues or heavy equipment).
• Airport Queues:
  - In case of long wait lines at the Enterprise counter, travelers should utilize Enterprise Emerald Club / National bypass or Alamo partner desks.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MEALS & INCIDENTAL EXPENSES (M&IE) PER DIEM
• Daily Per Diem Allowances:
  - Breakfast: $20.00
  - Lunch: $25.00
  - Dinner: $45.00
  - Daily Maximum: $90.00 / day ($110.00 / day in Tier 1 Metro cities).
• Alcohol: Not reimbursable unless attending an authorized client entertainment dinner.
• Itemized receipts are mandatory for all individual transactions exceeding $25.00.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CONFERENCE & PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT BUDGETS
• Annual Allowance: $3,500 per full-time employee per fiscal year.
• "Use-It-or-Lose-It" Deadline: Unspent conference budgets expire on September 30 (end of Q3).
• Unallocated funds may be reallocated across project codes with Department Head approval.
